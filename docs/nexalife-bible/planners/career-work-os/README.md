# Career, Work & Professional Growth Operating System (CWOS)

The user's professional life in one environment: career planning, jobs, freelancing, projects, clients, skills, networking, applications, interviews, and career transitions. Written as the `NLB-CWOS-*` series per `NLB-00` Article VII, occupying Volume 29.

Home domain: **LD-03 Career** (`NLB-03`). Catalogue anchor: **PU-03-001 – PU-03-034** (`NLB-04`).

| 2 | Advanced Career Intelligence, Leadership, Entrepreneurship & Global Opportunity | [NLB-CWOS-002](./NLB-CWOS-002-advanced-career-intelligence-leadership-and-entrepreneurship.md) |

## Scope boundaries

| Boundary | Owner | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Professional relationships | **CWOS** | `NLB-RSOS-001` explicitly defers professional contacts here, with separate visibility from personal ones |
| Communication intelligence | **RSOS** | CWOS consumes `NLB-RSOS-002`'s extraction/drafting engine and adds career-specific routing |
| Career income | **FWOS** | CWOS produces income and freelance profitability; FWOS owns budgeting, tax, wealth |
| Running an organization | Future Business OS (LD-04) | CWOS's Entrepreneur Mode is career-scale; organization-scale operations belong to `NLB-04`'s PU-04-* catalogue |

**Two safety rules:** generated resumes, cover letters, and application answers may never invent employers, achievements, certifications, skills, or results; and match scores are always phrased as *"your profile matches N% of the selected criteria"* — never as a probability of being hired.
