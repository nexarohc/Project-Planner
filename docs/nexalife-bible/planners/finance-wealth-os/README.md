# Finance, Wealth & Financial Freedom Operating System (FWOS)

NexaLife's central financial intelligence layer: cash flow, budgeting, debt, investments, business finance, and long-term wealth architecture. Written as the `NLB-FWOS-*` series per `NLB-00` Article VII, occupying Volume 28.

Home domain: **LD-05 Finance** (`NLB-03`). Catalogue anchor: **PU-05-001 – PU-05-042** (`NLB-04`).

**Core philosophy:** money should serve your life, not control it. FWOS never treats accumulating money as the final objective — it connects to `NLB-BLOS-*`'s dream-funding pipeline so savings become experiences.

**Two safety boundaries worth noting:** contributions are always separated from market movement (so saving is never mistaken for investment performance), and Nexa never autonomously trades, transfers large sums, closes accounts, takes loans, or changes beneficiaries — the Finance equivalent of HealthOS's medication rule.
