# Relationships & Social Life Operating System (RSOS)

The People Graph, family and friends, communication intelligence, communities, and relationship automation. Written as the `NLB-RSOS-*` series per `NLB-00` Article VII, occupying Volume 27.

Home domains: **LD-08 Family**, **LD-10 Community** (`NLB-03`). Catalogue anchors: **PU-01-047**, **PU-08-\***, **PU-10-001–006** (`NLB-04`).

**Design constraint:** RSOS supports relationships, never manipulates them. It explicitly rejects relationship scoring, popularity metrics, guilt-driven notifications, and manipulative streaks, and prohibits inferring sensitive personal characteristics (health, politics, orientation, religion) from ordinary interactions.
