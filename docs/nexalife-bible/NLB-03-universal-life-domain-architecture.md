## Purpose

Instead of building hundreds of disconnected planners, NexaLife organizes everything into **Life Domains**.

Each domain becomes a complete ecosystem. Every ecosystem contains:

- Specialized AI
- Planners
- Dashboards
- Automation
- Analytics
- Community
- Templates
- Marketplace
- Voice
- Reports
- Goals
- Calendar
- Knowledge
- Documents
- Widgets

Every future feature belongs inside one or more domains. This keeps the platform organized, scalable, and understandable.

> **v1.1 note.** LD-11–LD-17 were added by amendment after the initial ten domains proved insufficient for professional and specialist planning (competitive sport, software delivery, property, farming, public service, formal research, and personal style/home). Two candidates considered during the same review — a standalone "Events" domain and a standalone "Pets" domain — were deliberately **not** added: event planning is a capability that recurs inside Family, Business, and Community rather than a domain of its own, and pet care is treated as part of Family life (see PU-08-036–038 in NLB-04). Per Article IV of NLB-00, a domain is added only when a planner genuinely fits nowhere else — not to make the catalogue look larger.

## Universal Module Structure

Every module, regardless of domain, follows the same architecture.

Each module includes:

- Dashboard
- Planner
- Calendar
- Goals
- Tasks
- AI Assistant
- Analytics
- Reports
- Documents
- Templates
- Automation
- Notifications
- Widgets
- Collaboration
- History
- Activity Log
- Settings
- Search
- Voice
- Offline Support
- Import / Export
- API Access (where appropriate)

This consistency reduces learning time and simplifies development.

---

## Cross-Domain Intelligence

One of NexaLife's biggest differentiators will be its ability to connect domains.

Examples:

- A **marathon goal** influences the Fitness Planner, Nutrition Planner, Sleep Planner, Calendar, and Budget Planner.
- An **exam schedule** updates the Study Planner, Calendar, Habit Planner, and Focus Sessions.
- A **family vacation** coordinates the Travel Planner, Budget Planner, Calendar, and Shared Tasks.

Users shouldn't have to duplicate information across modules.

---

## Universal AI Orchestrator

Above all specialist AI systems sits **Nexa**.

Nexa acts as the orchestrator. Responsibilities:

- Understand user intent.
- Delegate work to the correct specialist AI.
- Coordinate results across domains.
- Respect user permissions.
- Maintain context where appropriate.
- Recommend automations.
- Surface insights without overwhelming the user.

---

## Design Rule

Every future feature must answer:

1. Which Life Domain does it belong to?
2. Which AI specialists support it?
3. Which other domains should it interact with?
4. Does it fit the Universal Module Structure?

If those questions can't be answered, the feature needs further design.
